Description

Chicken Curry is a flavorful and comforting dish made with chicken cooked in a blend of onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and aromatic spices. It is easy to prepare and can be served with rice, roti, or naan.

Ingredients

  • 500 g mutton
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 medium onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt
  • 1 cup water
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Clean and cut the chicken into medium-sized pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add chopped onion and cook until golden.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Add tomatoes and spices.
  6. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  7. Add chicken and mix well.
  8. Cook for a few minutes.
  9. Add water and cover the pan.
  10. Cook until the chicken is completely cooked.
  11. Add garam masala and mix.
  12. Garnish with coriander leaves.
  13. Serve hot with rice, roti, or naan.
